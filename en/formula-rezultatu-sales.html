What might be holding you back

I've already taken lots of courses and nothing changed
That's one of the signs that one of your components is at zero. Courses give knowledge, but if B (beliefs) or D (desire) is off, no amount of knowledge turns into results. Here we find the glitch first, and only then get to work.
Isn't this just another “motivational” program?
No. Motivation can't be cured with motivation. This is neuroscience and concrete mechanics: why the brain sabotages, how the β loop works, why your environment affects you more than you think. No “believe in yourself”.
How much time does it take?
The lecture takes about 40 minutes. Each workbook, 20-30 minutes. 3-4 hours in total, spread out at whatever pace suits you. Access never expires, so come back whenever you like.
What if it doesn't work for me?
Write to me. We'll figure it out together. If the material truly gave you nothing new, we'll talk about it honestly.
